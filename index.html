Before you enter

Who can take part?

Students from any college and branch. No prior experience needed for most trials.

Can I join more than one event?

Yes. Check the schedule so your trials don't overlap.

How do teams work?

Team size is on each event stone. Every member must register.

Is there a registration fee?

Fees are shown at registration. Dragon Hack and Bot Forge have a per-team fee.

Claim your seat

Registration is open to all branches. Pick a team size, pick your trials, show up.
